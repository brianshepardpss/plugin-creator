PASS only if ALL hold:
- 4-6 named, job-related competencies (no "culture fit", personality or
  "energy" competency).
- For each competency: at least 2 behavioral questions and at least 1
  situational question, each with probes.
- An anchored 1-4 rubric per competency, where anchors describe observable
  evidence rather than adjectives.
- A panel split across the CEO, head of product and advisor in which no
  competency is owned by two people (a clearly labeled "secondary" check is
  acceptable).
- No question touches age, family, national origin, citizenship (work
  authorization is fine), religion, disability/health, arrests or salary
  history.

FAIL otherwise.
