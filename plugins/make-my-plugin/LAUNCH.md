# Launch plan: Make My Plugin (Level 4)

The question this answers is bigger than "is this plugin wanted": do people
who are not developers want custom AI assistants built from their own work,
and will they pay someone to build one for them?

- Positioning: "Your way of doing it, as a one-click Claude assistant. No
  coding. Built from your own past examples."
- Audience: small-business owners and solo professionals already paying for
  Claude (Pro/Max/Team) who use Cowork.
- Channels:
  - r/ClaudeAI (post a before/after: three old letters in, assistant out).
  - r/smallbusiness and r/sweatystartup: answer "how do you use AI in your
    business" threads with the deposit-letter example; no cold posts.
  - LinkedIn: a 60-second screen recording of the sample flow.
  - Cowork-focused communities and newsletters.
- Waitlist test (the Level 4 signal): the landing page in docs/ (built-for-you.html) offers a
  "built for you" service -- send three examples, get a tested plugin back.
  Count signups before building any service.
- Listing text: "Make My Plugin -- turn a task you repeat into your own
  one-click Claude assistant, learned from your past examples. No code."
- Day-30 signal: 40+ .plugin downloads from releases, 10+ community-plugin
  submissions or "here's what I built" replies, and 25+ waitlist signups for
  the done-for-you version. Stop signal: under 10 downloads.
