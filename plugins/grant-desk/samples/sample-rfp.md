# Cedar Mill Family Foundation - 2027 Community Literacy Grants (SAMPLE RFP)

Grant Desk researches a real Waco-area private foundation as a stand-in

## About the opportunity

The Cedar Mill Family Foundation supports nonprofits in Central Texas that
help children in grades K-3 build reading skills. We fund direct service
programs with measurable outcomes.

- Award range: $10,000 to $25,000 for one year. Requests above $25,000 will
  not be reviewed.
- Geography: McLennan, Bell, Coryell and Falls counties, Texas.
- Eligibility: 501(c)(3) public charities with an annual budget under $2
  million.
- Grant period: February 1, 2027 to January 31, 2028.

## Key dates

- Letter of inquiry (LOI) due: October 30, 2026, 5:00 p.m. Central
- Invited full proposals due: November 20, 2026, 5:00 p.m. Central
- Award notification: January 15, 2027
- Interim report due: August 15, 2027
- Final report due: February 28, 2028

## Use of AI tools

Applicants may use AI writing tools to help prepare proposals. If you do,
check the disclosure box on the cover sheet and describe briefly how AI was
used. Applicants are responsible for the accuracy of every statement.

## Proposal questions

Answer each question within its word limit. Limits are strict; text beyond
the limit is not read.

### Q1. Organization and need (300 words)
Describe your organization and the reading need among the children you serve.
Include data on the population served.

### Q2. Program design (400 words)
Describe the program this grant would support: who participates, what they
do, how often, and who delivers it.

### Q3. Outcomes and evaluation (300 words)
What results do you expect in the grant year, and how will you measure them?
Include last year's results if available.

### Q4. Budget and sustainability (200 words)
How will the grant be used, what is the total program budget, and how will
the program be sustained after this grant?

## Required attachments

1. Program budget (use the foundation's one-page template)
2. IRS determination letter
3. Board list with affiliations
4. Most recent audit or financial statements
