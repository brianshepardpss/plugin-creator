## Mission

Riverbend Youth Literacy helps children in kindergarten through third grade
in McLennan County read on grade level by pairing them with trained volunteer
reading buddies and putting books in their homes.

## Programs

### Reading Buddies (core program)
Trained adult volunteers meet one-on-one with a K-3 student twice a week for
30 minutes during the school day, using a structured phonics-and-fluency
curriculum. Partner campuses supply space and refer students who score below
benchmark on the fall screener.

### Summer Book Bridge
Six-week summer program at partner library branches: read-alouds, a take-home
book each week, and a family reading log.

### Family Literacy Nights
Evening events at partner campuses where caregivers learn simple at-home
reading routines and each family takes home books.

### Why early reading (approved 2026-07)

steeper path through school. Our work focuses on the K-3 window, when
one-on-one practice can change a child's trajectory.
