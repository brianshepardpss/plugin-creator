# Funder AI-use policies (reference for the grant-draft gate)

Read this when choosing DRAFT or ASSIST mode. Policies change; when the
user's RFP or funder website says something different, the funder's current
text wins. Verified dates are when Grant Desk last checked the source.

## Modes

- DRAFT: the funder permits AI-assisted drafting (often with disclosure).
  Grant Desk may write section drafts from the org profile, every claim
  tagged, for the user to rewrite and own.
- ASSIST: the funder prohibits or restricts AI-generated application text,
  or the user does not know and prefers to be safe. Grant Desk produces only:
  the question map, an outline of points per question (bullets that name
  which profile facts to use, not finished sentences), critique and line
  edits of text the user wrote, fact and word-limit checks, and the tracker.
  It does not produce submit-ready prose for any section.

## Restricted or conditional funders (verified)

| Funder | Rule (quoted or closely paraphrased) | Mode | Source (checked) |
|---|---|---|---|
| NIH (all institutes) | NOT-OD-25-132: NIH "will not consider applications that are either substantially developed by AI, or contain sections substantially developed by AI, to be original ideas of applicants." Post-award detection may be referred to the Office of Research Integrity. Also caps a PI at six new, renewal, resubmission or revision applications per calendar year. Effective for the September 25, 2025 receipt date and later. | ASSIST | https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-25-132.html (2026-10-02) |
| Spencer Foundation | Applicants may "use generative AI as a resource to support the production of content" but may not "submit verbatim drafts of content generated by AI"; AI must be "an assistive tool rather than as a substitute author"; disclosure checkbox describing where, how and why AI was used. | ASSIST (outline, critique, edits; the user writes the prose) | https://www.spencer.org/resources/policy-on-the-use-of-generative-ai-at-the-spencer-foundation (2026-10-02) |

Treat any other federal research sponsor (NSF, DOE, NEH, IES) as "check the
current notice" and ask the user; do not assume either way.

## Signals in an RFP

Search the RFP text for: AI, artificial intelligence, generative,
ChatGPT, large language model, machine-generated, "original work",
"written by the applicant", disclosure. Quote the sentence you find.

- "may use AI ... disclose" -> DRAFT, and add the disclosure sentence.
- "must not", "will not consider", "prohibited", "verbatim", "substantially
  developed by AI", "original work of the applicant" -> ASSIST.
- Silent -> ask the user. If they do not know, recommend asking the program
  officer; offer ASSIST now, or DRAFT only if the user confirms they accept
  the risk, and include the disclosure sentence.

## Disclosure sentence (offer in DRAFT mode)

"We used an AI writing assistant (Claude, with the Grant Desk plugin) to
organize our existing program materials and produce a first draft. Staff
rewrote and verified every statement, and all figures come from our own
records."
