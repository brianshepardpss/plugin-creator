RESIDENTIAL PURCHASE AGREEMENT (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)
Sample form for software demos only. Not a state association form. Not for use in a real
transaction.

1. PARTIES. Seller: Dana Samplewood and Rowan Samplewood. Buyer: Avery Sampleton.
2. PROPERTY. 26 Larkspur Dr, Cedar Hollow, TX 75999, Lot 14, Block C, Cedar Hollow Estates
(fictional).
3. SALES PRICE. Total sales price: $405,000. Cash portion: $81,000. Third-party financing:
$324,000 (conventional loan).
4. EFFECTIVE DATE. The Effective Date is the date of final acceptance shown on the
signature page: October 9, 2026.
5. EARNEST MONEY AND OPTION FEE. Within 3 business days after the Effective Date, Buyer
shall deliver earnest money of $4,000 and an option fee of $250 to Fictional Title
Company, 100 Example Plaza, Cedar Hollow, TX 75999.
6. INSPECTION (OPTION) PERIOD. Buyer may terminate this contract for any reason by notice
to Seller within 10 days after the Effective Date.
7. SELLER'S DISCLOSURE. Seller shall deliver the Seller's Disclosure Notice within 5 days
after the Effective Date.
8. HOA DOCUMENTS. Seller shall deliver the subdivision information (HOA documents) within
5 business days after the Effective Date.
9. TITLE. Seller shall furnish a title commitment within 15 days after the Effective Date.
Buyer may object to title matters within 5 business days after the title commitment
deadline.
10. SURVEY. Seller shall deliver an existing survey or Buyer shall obtain a new survey
within 20 days after the Effective Date.
11. APPRAISAL. If the property appraises for less than the sales price, Buyer may
terminate by notice within 21 days after the Effective Date.
12. FINANCING. If Buyer cannot obtain loan approval, Buyer may terminate by notice within
25 days after the Effective Date.
13. CLOSING. Closing shall occur on or before November 13, 2026.
14. FINAL WALK-THROUGH. Buyer may conduct a final walk-through no later than 1 day before
the Closing Date.
15. POSSESSION. Seller shall deliver possession at closing and funding.
16. TIME. All deadlines end at 5:00 p.m. local time at the property.
17. COMPUTATION OF DAYS. "Days" means calendar days unless "business days" is stated. A
business day is any day except Saturday, Sunday, or a federal legal holiday. If a deadline
falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or federal legal holiday, the deadline extends to the next
business day. The Effective Date itself is not counted.
18. NOTICES. Notices go to the addresses on the signature page or by email to the parties'
agents.
19. AGENTS. Listing broker: Example Realty Group (fictional). Buyer's broker: Placeholder
Homes LLC (fictional). Broker compensation is governed by separate written agreements and
is not part of this contract.
