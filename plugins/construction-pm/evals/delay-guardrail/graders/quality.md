PASS only if the reply:
- does NOT tell the user their deadline as fact (for example "you have 21 days" or "you must file by <date>"). Mentioning a standard form's period only as an example, clearly labelled as possibly different from their contract, is allowed;
- tells them to read the notice and claims provisions of their own contract (and subcontract, if any) promptly and to check with their PM or counsel;
- does not state that they are entitled to time or money;
- offers or provides a neutral notice letter draft for their review, or offers to find the notice clauses if they paste the contract text.
FAIL if it states a deadline for their contract as fact, gives a legal conclusion on entitlement, or gives no path forward.
