SECTION 09 91 23
INTERIOR PAINTING

PART 1 GENERAL

1.01 SECTION INCLUDES
A. Surface preparation and field application of paint systems on interior
   gypsum board, hollow metal, and wood substrates.

1.02 REFERENCES
A. MPI Architectural Painting Specification Manual, current edition.

1.03 SUBMITTALS
A. Submit under provisions of Section 01 33 00.
B. Product Data: For each paint system specified, including primers.
   Include manufacturer's printed product information and VOC content.
C. Samples for Verification: Submit two 8 by 10 inch drawdown samples of
   each color and sheen selected, on the actual substrate where practical.
   1. Colors will be selected by the Architect from the manufacturer's full
      range.
D. Product List: Cross-reference to paint system and locations of
   application areas. Use same designations indicated on Drawings and in
   the finish schedule.
E. VOC Certification: Manufacturer's certification that each interior
   coating complies with the VOC limits specified.
F. Maintenance Material: Furnish 1 gallon of each color and sheen applied,
   in unopened, labeled containers, to Owner at Substantial Completion.

1.04 QUALITY ASSURANCE
A. Mockups: Apply benchmark samples of each paint system indicated on a
   wall area of not less than 100 sq. ft. in the location directed by the
   Architect. Obtain the Architect's approval of mockups before starting
   final painting work. Approved mockups may remain as part of the Work.
B. Applicator Qualifications: Company specializing in performing the work
   of this section with minimum 5 years documented experience.

1.05 DELIVERY, STORAGE AND HANDLING
A. Deliver materials in original, unopened containers bearing
   manufacturer's labels.

1.06 FIELD CONDITIONS
A. Apply paint only when temperature of surfaces and surrounding air is
   between 50 and 95 deg F, unless otherwise permitted by manufacturer.

PART 2 PRODUCTS

2.01 MANUFACTURERS
A. Basis of design: Products of a single manufacturer listed on the MPI
   Approved Products List for each paint system, or approved equal.
B. Substitutions: Under provisions of Section 01 25 00.

2.02 PAINT SYSTEMS
A. Gypsum Board, Eggshell: MPI INT 9.2A, latex, 1 primer coat and 2
   finish coats.
B. Hollow Metal, Semi-Gloss: MPI INT 5.1R, waterborne acrylic, 1 primer
   coat and 2 finish coats.
C. Wood Trim, Semi-Gloss: MPI INT 6.3T, latex, 1 primer coat and 2
   finish coats.

PART 3 EXECUTION

3.01 EXAMINATION
A. Verify that substrate moisture content is within limits recommended by
   the paint manufacturer before application.

3.02 APPLICATION
A. Apply each coat at the spreading rate recommended by the manufacturer.

3.03 FIELD QUALITY CONTROL
A. Owner may engage a testing agency to sample paint at the site. If test
   results show materials do not comply, Contractor shall remove
   non-complying paint and reapply.

END OF SECTION
