SECTION 23 05 93 - TESTING, ADJUSTING, AND BALANCING FOR HVAC

PART 1 - GENERAL

1.1 SUMMARY
A. Section includes testing, adjusting, and balancing (TAB) of air
   systems serving the tenant improvement area, including rooftop unit
   RTU-2, VAV terminal units, and exhaust fans EF-1 and EF-2.

1.2 DEFINITIONS
A. TAB: Testing, adjusting, and balancing.
B. TAB Specialist: An independent entity meeting qualifications to
   perform TAB work.

1.3 ACTION SUBMITTALS
A. None required for this Section.

1.4 INFORMATIONAL SUBMITTALS
A. Qualification Data: Within 30 days after Notice to Proceed, submit
   documentation that the TAB Specialist and this Project's TAB team
   members meet the qualifications specified in "Quality Assurance"
   Article.
B. Contract Documents Examination Report: Within 45 days after Notice to
   Proceed, submit the Contract Documents review report as specified in
   Part 3.
C. Strategies and Procedures Plan: Within 60 days after Notice to Proceed,
   submit TAB strategies and step-by-step procedures as specified in
   "Preparation" Article.
D. Certified TAB Reports: Submit two copies of the certified TAB report,
   signed by the TAB Specialist.
E. Instrument Calibration Reports, including:
   1. Instrument type and make.
   2. Serial number.
   3. Date of calibration.

1.5 QUALITY ASSURANCE
A. TAB Specialist Qualifications: Certified by AABC or NEBB.
B. Certify TAB field data reports and perform the following:
   1. Review field data reports to validate accuracy of data.
   2. Certify that the TAB team complied with the approved TAB plan.

PART 2 - PRODUCTS (Not Applicable)

PART 3 - EXECUTION

3.1 EXAMINATION
A. Examine the Contract Documents to become familiar with Project
   requirements and to discover conditions in systems designs that may
   preclude proper TAB of systems and equipment.

3.2 PREPARATION
A. Prepare a TAB plan that includes strategies and step-by-step
   procedures for balancing the systems.

3.3 FINAL REPORT
A. Final TAB Report: Submit the final report for Architect and Engineer
   review before Substantial Completion. Include a certification sheet,
   system diagrams, and measured versus design airflow for each outlet.
