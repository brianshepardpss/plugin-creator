SECTION 08 71 00 - DOOR HARDWARE

PART 1 - GENERAL

1.1 RELATED DOCUMENTS
A. Drawings and general provisions of the Contract, including General and
   Supplementary Conditions and other Division 01 Specification Sections,
   apply to this Section.

1.2 SUMMARY
A. Section includes mechanical and electrified door hardware for swinging
   doors, cylinders and keying, and door hardware for hollow metal and wood
   doors.
B. Related Sections:
   1. Section 08 11 13 "Hollow Metal Doors and Frames" for frame preparation
      and electric power transfer reinforcing.
   2. Section 28 13 00 "Access Control" for card readers and power supplies.

1.3 ACTION SUBMITTALS
A. Product Data: For each type of product indicated. Include construction
   details, material descriptions, dimensions, finishes, and operating
   characteristics.
B. Door Hardware Schedule: Prepared by or under the supervision of the
   Architectural Hardware Consultant. Coordinate with the door and frame
   schedules and with final hollow metal shop drawings.
   1. Submit concurrently with Section 08 11 13 shop drawings.
   2. Use vertical format, listing each door opening and hardware set.
C. Wiring Diagrams: For electrified door hardware, include elevation of each
   electrified opening, riser diagrams, point-to-point wiring, and power
   supply locations. Coordinate with Section 28 13 00.
D. Keying Schedule: Prepared under the supervision of the Owner, the
   Architect and the Architectural Hardware Consultant, after a keying
   conference with the Owner.
E. Samples: For each exposed product in each finish specified, in
   manufacturer's standard size. Samples will be returned to Contractor.

1.4 INFORMATIONAL SUBMITTALS
A. Qualification Data: For Architectural Hardware Consultant.
B. Product Certificates: Signed by manufacturers certifying that products
   comply with ANSI/BHMA Grade 1 requirements indicated.

1.5 CLOSEOUT SUBMITTALS
A. Maintenance Data: For door hardware to include in maintenance manuals.
B. Warranty: Sample of special warranty.
C. Final keying schedule and bitting list, delivered to Owner by
   registered mail.

1.6 QUALITY ASSURANCE
A. Architectural Hardware Consultant Qualifications: A person who is
   currently certified as an Architectural Hardware Consultant and who is
   experienced in providing consulting services for door hardware
   installations comparable in material, design and extent to this Project.
B. Keying Conference: Conduct conference at Project site. Incorporate
   keying conference decisions into final keying schedule after reviewing
   door hardware keying system including existing building master key
   system.

1.7 DELIVERY, STORAGE AND HANDLING
A. Deliver keys and permanent cores to Owner by registered mail or
   overnight package service.
B. Lead Time: Electrified mortise locksets and electric power transfers
   are long-lead items. Allow 10 to 12 weeks after approval of submittals
   for manufacture and delivery. Order immediately upon approval.

1.8 WARRANTY
A. Special Warranty: Manufacturer agrees to repair or replace components
   of door hardware that fail in materials or workmanship within specified
   warranty period.
   1. Warranty Period: Five years from date of Substantial Completion for
      mortise locks; two years for electrified hardware.

PART 2 - PRODUCTS

2.1 MANUFACTURERS
A. Basis of Design: Products named in the hardware sets are the basis of
   design. Comparable products of other manufacturers may be considered,
   or approved equal, in accordance with Division 01 Section "Substitution
   Procedures."

2.2 HINGES
A. Butts and Hinges: BHMA A156.1, five-knuckle, ball bearing, 4-1/2 by
   4-1/2 inches, non-removable pin at out-swinging exterior doors.

2.3 MECHANICAL AND ELECTRIFIED LOCKS
A. Mortise Locks: BHMA A156.13, Grade 1, lever trim, 2-3/4 inch backset.
B. Electrified Mortise Locks: BHMA A156.13, Grade 1, 24 VDC, fail-secure
   unless otherwise indicated, with request-to-exit switch.
   1. Submit product data for electrified locks with the wiring diagrams
      required in Article 1.3.

2.4 ELECTRIC POWER TRANSFERS
A. Concealed electric power transfer (EPT), 12 wire minimum, mortised into
   door and frame. Frame shall be prepared and reinforced for EPT under
   Section 08 11 13.

2.5 CLOSERS AND STOPS
A. Surface Closers: BHMA A156.4, Grade 1, cast iron body, adjustable
   spring power.
B. Wall Stops: BHMA A156.16, cast brass.

2.6 FINISHES
A. Finish: BHMA 626 (satin chromium plated) unless otherwise indicated.

PART 3 - EXECUTION

3.1 EXAMINATION
A. Examine doors and frames, with Installer present, for compliance with
   requirements for installation tolerances, labeled fire-rated door
   assembly construction, wall and floor construction, and other
   conditions affecting performance.

3.2 INSTALLATION
A. Mount hardware units at heights indicated in DHI "Recommended Locations
   for Architectural Hardware for Standard Steel Doors and Frames."
B. Install electrified hardware and connect to the access control system
   wiring provided under Section 28 13 00.

3.3 FIELD QUALITY CONTROL
A. Independent Architectural Hardware Consultant Inspection: After
   installation, the Architectural Hardware Consultant shall inspect each
   opening and submit a written inspection report to the Architect listing
   deficiencies.
