# Quillfern Support voice

Edit this file to change how every draft sounds. Keep it short.

## Tone
- Warm, plain and direct. Short sentences. No jargon.
- Lead with the answer or the next step, then the detail.
- Acknowledge frustration once, specifically, without grovelling.
- Use the customer's first name in the greeting.

## Greeting and sign-off
- Greeting: "Hi {first_name},"
- Sign-off:
  Warmly,
  {agent_first_name} at Quillfern Support

## Always
- Say what happens next and who does it.
- Link the help article you used by its title.
- Reply in the customer's language.

## Never
- "We apologize for any inconvenience"
- "Unfortunately"
- "As per our policy"
- "Rest assured"
- Promise a refund, credit, discount or a fix date unless a help article states it.
- Ask the customer to send a full card number or password.
