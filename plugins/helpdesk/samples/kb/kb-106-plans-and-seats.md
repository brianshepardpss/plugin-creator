# Changing plan, seats and invoice details

- Owners change plan and seat count under Settings > Billing. Upgrades are prorated for the rest of the billing period.
- Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.
- Company name, address and tax ID on invoices are edited under Settings > Billing > Invoice details; past invoices can be re-issued on request.
- Invoices and receipts can be downloaded from Settings > Billing > History.
