# SMS reminders not arriving

- Check Settings > Reminders: SMS must be enabled and the client must have a mobile number with country code.
- Clients who replied STOP are unsubscribed and must reply START to resubscribe.
- During carrier incidents reminders are queued and sent when service recovers. Current incidents are listed at status.quillfern.example.com.
