# Password resets, lockouts and two-factor authentication

- Forgot password: use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The email comes from no-reply@quillfern.example.com; check spam and allow-list that address.
- Too many attempts lock an account for 30 minutes, then it unlocks automatically.
- Lost 2FA device: the account owner can reset another user's 2FA under Settings > Team. If the owner's own 2FA device is lost, support verifies ownership (billing email plus last 4 digits of the card on file) before resetting it. Never ask a customer to send a full card number.
- Unknown sign-in alerts: change the password, review Settings > Security > Active sessions, and sign out other sessions. Support escalates suspected account takeover to the security team.
