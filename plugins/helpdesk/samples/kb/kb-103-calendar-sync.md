# Troubleshooting Google Calendar and Outlook sync

1. Disconnect and reconnect the calendar under Settings > Integrations.
2. Re-authorize access for each staff member whose calendar is not syncing.
3. Check the staff member's calendar time zone matches the business time zone.
4. Sync runs every 5 minutes; changes can take up to 10 minutes to appear.
5. If bookings still do not appear, download the sync log (Settings > Integrations > Download log) and attach it to your ticket.
Apple Calendar is not supported for two-way sync; use the read-only iCal feed link instead.
