# Adding staff and setting permissions

- Settings > Team > Add staff member. Each staff member uses one seat.
- Roles: Owner, Admin, Front desk (books for everyone, no reports), Practitioner (own schedule only).
- A new practitioner sees an empty schedule until they are assigned to at least one service and working hours are set under their profile.
