# Exporting bookings and client data

- Reports > Export lets the account owner or an admin download bookings, clients and payments as CSV.
- Choose a date range of up to 12 months per export.
- The price column shows the price at time of booking; bookings made with a package show 0.00 and the package name.
- Exports are emailed as a download link valid for 24 hours.
