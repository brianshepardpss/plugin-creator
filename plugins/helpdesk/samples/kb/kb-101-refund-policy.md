# Refunds and billing credits

- Monthly and annual charges can be refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of the charge date.
- Duplicate charges caused by a billing error are always refunded, whenever they are reported.
- After 14 days, charges are not refundable. A support lead may review the account and offer an account credit in exceptional cases; agents cannot promise a credit or its amount.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5-10 business days to appear.
- To request a review, the account owner replies to their support ticket; the support lead answers within 2 business days.
