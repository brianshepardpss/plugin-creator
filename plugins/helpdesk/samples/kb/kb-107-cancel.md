# Cancelling your subscription

- The account owner cancels under Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Service continues until the end of the paid period.
- Data stays available for export for 30 days after the plan ends, then is deleted.
- Cancellation does not refund the current period except under the refund policy (kb-101).
